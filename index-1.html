// 01 – WHY CCTV MATTERS

Importance of CCTV Cameras for Everyone

A CCTV (Closed-Circuit Television) system is no longer a luxury. It is the most affordable way to protect your family, property and business.

Crime, theft and disputes can happen in a moment – but a security camera never blinks. A properly installed CCTV system watches your property day and night, records everything, and gives you proof when you need it most.

What is CCTV?

CCTV is a system of cameras, a recorder (DVR or NVR), a hard disk and a monitor or mobile app. Cameras capture video, the recorder stores it, and you can watch it live or play back old footage whenever you want.

Why every home and business needs it

Visible cameras make thieves think twice. If something does happen, clear footage helps the police, your insurance company and you to find the truth quickly.

  • Crime prevention: cameras discourage theft, burglary and vandalism.
  • Evidence: recorded video proves what really happened.
  • Remote viewing: watch home or shop from your mobile anywhere.
  • Employee & customer safety: a safer, more disciplined workplace.
  • Child & elder care: keep an eye on loved ones at home.
  • Peace of mind: sleep and travel without worry.
  • Low cost: a one-time setup protects you for years.

// 05 – BUYER'S GUIDE

Choosing the Right CCTV System

Types of CCTV cameras

Dome cameras suit indoor areas and shops. Bullet cameras are great outdoors and are a strong visible deterrent. PTZ cameras can rotate and zoom to cover large areas. Wireless / Wi-Fi cameras are simple for small homes. IP cameras give the highest picture quality and flexible networking.

Things to consider before buying

Resolution (2MP, 4MP, 5MP and above), night vision range, weather protection for outdoor use, number of cameras, hard disk storage for how many days of recording you want, and a good power supply. A correct design matters more than the number of cameras – placing them at gates, entrances, cash counters and blind spots gives the best protection.

Tips to get the best from your CCTV

Keep lenses clean, use a UPS or backup power so recording continues during load-shedding, change default passwords, and check your recordings regularly. We help with all of this when you choose Sheikh Faizan CCTV.
